** networking (its own)
- a body in Brotli or Zstandard is read (Content-Encoding: br, zstd;
  tiny_libs' Brotli, with its dictionary, and Zstd, elm-playground
  after 0.3.3), and asked for: Accept-Encoding: gzip, br, zstd.
  Berkshire Hathaway, which answers in Brotli whatever is asked, loads
- cookies (RFC 6265): a response's Set-Cookie kept (Domain, Path,
  Expires and Max-Age, Secure, HttpOnly), said back in the Cookie
  header of the requests to its site, a redirection's before the next
  request (a sign-in); one jar for the browser, shared by the
  requests on threads (Cookie, Cookie_jar); document.cookie for the
  page's scripts; those with a date kept between runs in the
  profile's Cookies file (Browser_cookies), not with profile=off;
  about:cookies shows the jar. Not done: the Public Suffix List,
  SameSite
- libs/network: elm-playground's networking, the part a browser needs
  (Url, Urlencoded, Http; Asn1, Pem, X509, Tls13; Tcp, Worker,
  Tls_client, Http_client, Http_request), copied to grow here (cookies,
  compression, keep-alive); the cryptography stays tiny_libs'.
  Wrapped (the Playground links tiny_libs' modules of the same names)
  but opened by the libraries using it: the code says Http_request,
  not Network.Http_request
- src/chrome/Fetch: the requests in flight, after the Playground's
  native Commands.ml: http:// by Http_request stepped each frame,
  https:// by Http_client on Worker's threads (threads=off: the frame
  waits); a tab asks for a fetch by a message and never touches a
  socket, the program steps them on each Tick. The Playground no
  longer fetches anything for the browser
- gzip: "Accept-Encoding: gzip" sent, a body with "Content-Encoding:
  gzip" decompressed (Http.parse_response, over tiny_libs' Gzip, in
  elm-playground after 0.3.3): Wikipedia's article on OCaml comes as
  64 KB instead of 354
- tests/network, tests/network_unix: 49 tests (RFC 3986's table, RFC
  8448's TLS 1.3 trace, real certificate chains, a GET over localhost)

** tools
- the browsers before this one, from elm-playground, on the browser's
  own libraries (Fetch and our TLS for their requests, the same tab):
  mini-mosaic (NCSA Mosaic, 1993: the grey window, the globe, the
  stages of the pipeline as views), mini-netscape (1994-1997: its
  extensions, tables, CSS1, four connections at once), mini-firefox
  (2004: scripts, a console, the tree). tools/mosaic, netscape,
  firefox; tools/platform, the window they are linked with;
  scripts/tools/dump_old_browsers.sh dumps their pages
- scripts/js/Page_scripts.exe: a saved page's scripts run with no
  window, their console, requests and cookies said, a question asked
  of the page at the end
- mini-node: the JavaScript engine in a terminal, as Node.js is V8 in
  one (tools/node: Node_host, MiniNode). A file run as a module, -e a
  text, or a console; console, process (argv, env, exit, stdout),
  timers on the machine's clock and the loop that waits for them,
  require with CommonJS modules (a file run once, in a function that
  gives it exports, require and module), fs (readFileSync,
  writeFileSync, existsSync). Js_eval.eval_in_run and call_in_run: a
  host function called by a script running a text or a function
- mini-curl (tools/curl): a URL fetched and printed by the browser's
  own HTTP, TLS 1.3, gzip and cookies; -i the head, -L a redirection
  followed, -v each request and each answer's head, -d a POST, -o a
  file, -f. Http_client.once: one request, a redirection not followed
- mini-httpd (tools/httpd): a directory's files served on 127.0.0.1
  (-p port): a file's type from its extension, a directory's
  index.html or its listing, 301 to a directory's /, 404, 403 for
  what is above the root, 405 but for GET
- mini-lynx (tools/lynx): a page (an address or a file) as text, its
  links numbered; a number typed follows one, an address opens it, b
  comes back (-dump: no prompt; -w: the width). Line_mode: div,
  section, form, table and a table's row are lines of their own
- tests/tools, an eighth suite: the tools' worked examples, mini-curl
  and mini-lynx on mini-httpd's server forked on a temporary
  directory; Node_host's tests moved there
- docs/sites.md: sixteen sites, from the first web page to BBC News,
  and what each lacks by part (network, HTML, CSS, text, JavaScript),
  in colours; twelve real scripts the same way. 8 sites green, 4
  yellow, 4 red on 2026-10-02 (scripts/sites/dump_sites.sh to check
  again)
- make loc, make loc-v (scripts/stats/loc.py, after elm-playground's):
  the lines of OCaml and how much of the budget they are, 30,000 for
  languages/, libs/ and src/
- scripts/js/Js_survey.exe: real scripts parsed and run, the first
  mistake of each: what the JavaScript engine lacks, most common first

** speed
- libs/opti: Mini_opti.enabled, the switch between an optimized
  function and the simple one kept runnable beside it (after
  elm-playground's Opti); opti=off on the command line, and on
  scripts/perf/Page_bench.exe's. Nothing checks it yet
  (docs/plans/plan_performance.md, step 1)
- a line's shapes built when it is first shown, not the whole page's
  at each relayout (Browser_draw.later, between; opti=off: as before).
  Wikipedia's article on OCaml, 16,000 pixels high: a relayout 450 ms
  to 28, the page first shown in 239 ms instead of 796, its load
  settled in 6.2 s instead of 14.8 (docs/plans/plan_performance.md, step 2)
- a letter drawn as one picture made once (Glyph_picture: its opacity
  from the distance to the pen's strokes) instead of a rectangle a
  segment and a dot a point: a frame of about:chrome drawn in 8 ms
  instead of 74 (Cairo), 32 instead of 116 (software), and a scroll
  far smoother. letters=segments, or opti=off: the pen's way
  (docs/plans/plan_performance.md, step 4b)
- a Wikipedia article scrolled: a step from 340 ms to 15. Cairo's
  platform converted most of the letters' pictures again at each
  frame (it kept the last 32: elm-playground's now keeps them all, up
  to 16 million pixels), and the view read the page's every fragment
  three times a frame to find its players and controls
  (Browser_media.players_of, Browser_draw.controls_of: kept a layout).
  With the pen's segments it was 97, with opti=off 139
- a window at rest is not drawn again: Window_view.view gives back
  the frame before's list of shapes when the model is the same but
  for its time, and the platform skips the frame (run_app
  ~window's skip_same_view, elm-playground after 0.3.3). A frame at rest: 12 ms
  of CPU (about:blank) to 72 (about:chrome) before, 0.3 now; a page
  with a player is still drawn at each (docs/plans/plan_performance.md,
  step 4a)
- scripts/perf: Page_bench.exe (a page's stages timed) and
  load_timeline.sh (the real program's load in time)

  clicks and drags, what is typed made an address or a search, its
  drawing), and its text can be selected and edited as a line of text
  usually is (Gui_field: a caret and an anchor): a first click selects

  selects all; the arrows, Home and End move the caret, with Shift
  they select; typing, Backspace and Delete replace the selection;
  Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C, Ctrl+X, Ctrl+V, to and from the desktop's

  Playground_platform.clipboard, new in elm-playground)

  over a link, the I-beam over the omnibox and a page's text field,
  the arrow elsewhere (Window_layout.cursor_of; given to the platform
  by the main after each message: Playground_platform.set_cursor, new
  in elm-playground)
- a PDF viewer: a PDF file opens in a tab, its pages one under the
  other, drawn as they scroll into view (Pdf_viewer; about:pdf is a
  sample). libs/pdf: the file (objects, the table of objects as text
  or as a stream, packed objects, a file with no table; Flate with
  predictors, LZW, ASCII85, hexadecimal, run lengths), a page's
  operators (paths filled and stroked, clips, colours in the device,

  masks and JPEG, forms, axial and radial gradients), text in the
  four kinds of font. libs/fonts: TrueType, CFF (Type 2 charstrings)
  and Type 1 (its two ciphers, flex, accents) read to outlines; Type 3
  fonts run as content. A font not in the file is drawn with our
  stroke font at the standard widths. The flag pdf= turns each
  refinement off (pdf=strokes: our letters; pdf=plain: the simple
  path). Seven files of pdfTeX, cairo, Chrome and LibreOffice are
  drawn near poppler's pictures (tests/pdf); of 149 PDF files found
  on the machine it was written on, all are drawn
- about:tube shows every kind of media the browser plays: the clip
  also as MPEG-1 alone (our encoder); a section of sounds, an <audio>
  each -- two chirps as Opus (its file, a WebM), Vorbis, MP3, MP2,
  blips as a WAV, and a tune as a MIDI file, a tracker's module, ABC
  and solfege. A module plays in a page (rendered whole by its
  player: Media.module_sound). A test opens every file of the page
- Opus (libs/audio/opus), where it is CELT -- music, and what a file made
  for a page holds: Opus (a packet's first byte and its one to 48
  frames, OpusHead), Celt (a frame: the bands' energies, the
  allocation computed and not sent, each band's shape as pulses
  counted and numbered, splits, folding, two channels as mid and
  side, short blocks and the anti-collapse, the inverse MDCT with
  its short overlap, the pitch post-filter), Range_decoder. An .opus
  file and a WebM's Opus track play (Media). Five files of libopus's
  encoder decode to libopus's samples, a rounding apart. SILK and
  hybrid frames are silence of their length. Mdct: the transform of
  Vorbis and Opus in one module, its Fourier transform of any size

  its own decoders of pictures, video, sound and compression
