# -*- org -*-

* 0.1 (Q4 2026) (fork of elm-playground's TinyChrome, its own networking)
# unless mentioned otherwise, everything below was done by Claude Code
# (with direction from me)

** networking (its own)
- libs/network: elm-playground's networking, the part a browser needs
  (Url, Urlencoded, Http; Asn1, Pem, X509, Tls13; Tcp, Worker,
  Tls_client, Http_client, Http_request), copied to grow here (cookies,
  compression, keep-alive); the cryptography stays tiny_libs'.
  Wrapped (the Playground links tiny_libs' modules of the same names)
  but opened by the libraries using it: the code says Http_request,
  not Network.Http_request
- src/chrome/Fetch: the requests in flight, after the Playground's
  native Commands.ml: http:// by Http_request stepped each frame,
  https:// by Http_client on Worker's threads (threads=off: the frame
  waits); a tab asks for a fetch by a message and never touches a
  socket, the program steps them on each Tick. The Playground no
  longer fetches anything for the browser
- tests/network, tests/network_unix: 49 tests (RFC 3986's table, RFC
  8448's TLS 1.3 trace, real certificate chains, a GET over localhost)

** Features
- everything TinyChrome had in elm-playground 0.3.0: HTML read as
  the WHATWG's tokenizer and tree builder do, CSS's cascade (@media,
  var(), calc(), the attributes' hints) and computed styles, CSS 2.1's
  box model (floats, inline-blocks, positioning, tables, lists) and
  flexbox, SVG pictures, PNG/GIF/JPEG, a small JavaScript (ES5 core)
  and its DOM, <video> and <audio> (MPEG-1 and MP2, MP3, AVI, FLC,
  Y4M, GIF), tabs, the omnibox (an address, or words searched on
  Wikipedia or DuckDuckGo), developer tools (Elements, Network)
- the built-in site: about:chrome, the earlier browsers' pages, and
  about:tube, a video site of our own (its clips in Tube_clips,
  from TinyMediaPlayer's Our_media)
- renamed MiniChrome (the window's title, about:chrome), no longer
  held to TinyChrome's budget of 5,000 lines
- the window resized (dragged, full screen, -size WxH): the page laid
  out again at its width, the chrome placed from the window's edges,
  instead of a 1000 by 1000 picture scaled with black bars (both
  programs, over the Playground's run_app ~screen_follows_window, of
  0.3.1); the window starts at 1280 by 900
- the page zoomed, as in Chrome: Ctrl and + (or =), Ctrl and -, Ctrl
  and the wheel, Ctrl and 0 back to 100%, by Chrome's steps (25% to
  500%); the whole page, laid out at the window's width divided by the
  zoom and drawn scaled; a zoom is its site's (every page and tab of
  that host) and shows in the omnibox
- the page's letters drawn with a round pen (Stroke_text: a dot at
  each point of a stroke): zoomed, the square corners of the segments
  stuck out of every joint, spikes on the bold most of all
- a right click on the page opens a menu, as Chrome's (Browser_menu):
  on a link, Open link in new tab (behind the tab shown) and Inspect;
  elsewhere Back, Forward, Reload and Inspect (the developer tools on
  the element under the pointer); kept inside the window near its edges
- libs/gui: the pieces of the chrome that know nothing of a browser,
  as values a Model-View-Update program keeps and draws: Gui_menu (a
  menu opened at a point: its place, its shapes, the item under a
  click) and Gui_text (text in cells); Browser_menu says only what the
  menu offers. Then, moved out of MiniChrome.ml, the same pixels:
  Gui_tabs (the strip of tabs), Gui_toolbar (Back, Forward, Reload,
  Stop), the status bubble (Gui_text), the colours (Gui_kit)
- docs/architecture.md: the Model-View-Update loop, the pieces of
  libs/gui and their style among the four of elm-playground's
  notes_gui.md, the one process and its pool of four threads next to
  Chrome's processes, the capabilities, what is kept between runs
- the profile (Browser_profile), what is kept from one run to the
  next: ~/.config/mini-chrome/Preferences, JSON as Chrome's, for now
  the window's size (it opens as it was left) and each site's zoom,
  written a second after one changes and when the program ends; profile=DIR for another directory, profile=off for
  none; a file that is not JSON is reported and left alone.
  The first files the program reads and writes, by Cap.open_in and
  Cap.open_out (and Cap.env for the directory) handed down from
  Cap.main, as Cap.network is for sockets
- -v (or -verbose): each file and URL opened said on the terminal, by
  Logs.info -- the profile's file read and written, the TLS roots'
  file, each request (GET or POST) and its answer (status, bytes, a
  redirection), a built-in page; -debug and -quiet as well (the
  Playground's flags, the names of xix's programs)
- languages/json: JSON read (elm-playground's Json, over our
  JavaScript lexer: comments and a last comma taken) and written

** Bug fixes
- the wheel scrolled the wrong way
- a q typed anywhere (the omnibox, a page's field) quit the browser:
  the Playground's key, on every native program. Fixed in
  elm-playground after 0.3.1, where the platform's keys are Ctrl + a
  key: Ctrl+Q quits here too (nothing changed in this repository; with
  0.3.1 installed, q still quits)
- the program ended (Cairo's INVALID_MATRIX) on a picture drawn with no
  width or no height: Hacker News' comment pages, which indent with
  <img src="s.gif" width=0>; a player squeezed to nothing

** build infra
- one opam package, mini-chrome, standing on elm-playground's packages
  (0.3.1): the Playground for its window and drawing
  (elm_playground, elm_playground_native: SDL and Cairo), tiny_libs for
  the pictures, sound and video decoders
- ./configure (after elm-playground's): checks for SDL2 and Cairo,
  finds elm-playground's packages (from opam, or a 'make install' in an
  elm-playground checkout, or else pinned from the 0.3.1 tag)
- no Cairo needed: two programs, mini-chrome (Cairo's platform when
  installed, else the Playground's own rasterizer, by dune's select)
  and mini-chrome-software (always the rasterizer); ./configure
  --software leaves Cairo out, as it does by itself without Cairo
- bin/ symlink to _build/install/default/bin: ./bin/mini-chrome

** test infra
