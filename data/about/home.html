The web of 1993

This page is written as pages were in 1993, when NCSA Mosaic made the World Wide Web popular: HTML 2.0, its tags in capitals, no style sheet, no script. MiniChrome reads it the way Mosaic did: the bytes, then the tags, then a tree, then the looks, then the lines.

Everything it takes to show it is written from scratch:

  • the protocol, HTTP/1.1, and the address, the URL;
  • the encodings, Latin-1 and UTF-8 (café, naïve, “quotes”);
  • the images, GIF, PNG and JPEG;
  • the letters, drawn with the pen strokes of A. V. Hershey (1967).
