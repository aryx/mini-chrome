Pages that are programs

(if you can read this, the page's script did not run)

Mosaic read pages; Netscape made them look like something; since Netscape 2 (1995) a page is also a program, written in JavaScript, that the browser runs. The pages below are small programs, run by MiniChrome's own JavaScript engine. Click, type, and look inside as Firefox (2004) taught people to: the developer tools (F12, or a right click and Inspect) show the page's tree as the scripts leave it.

Pages that do something

  • A counter: a click, a number changed -- three ways to listen to a click
  • A to-do list: elements made by the script, a class toggled, CSS crossing it out
  • A stopwatch: setInterval, the page changing on its own
  • Tic-tac-toe: one handler for the whole board
